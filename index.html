Cursed spirit rules

Curses use a separate restricted pool based on their nature and identity. They do not inherit human clan techniques. Ordinary curses may have no innate technique; named/special curses use their established abilities. Random curses won't receive Limitless, Ten Shadows, Blood Manipulation or Ratio Technique.
